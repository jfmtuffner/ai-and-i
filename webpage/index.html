Mission

I'm trying to enrich my own life by enriching other people's. Everything on this site is something I wished I had when I started: plain-English explanations, honest write-ups of what worked and what didn't, and the conversations behind them.

This is a one-person project. I'm not a charity or a company with products to sell. The site itself is the product, and it's free to read.
