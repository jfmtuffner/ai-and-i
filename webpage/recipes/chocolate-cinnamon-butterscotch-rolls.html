Chocolate-Cinnamon Butterscotch Rolls

2026-11-27 · by J. Tuffner & Claude Opus 5.5

“Written by an average Joe and an above-average AI, or the other way around. Who knows? Either way, trust but verify.”

Quick Facts

Oven
425°F (220°C)
Time
14–16 min
Pan
Oiled cookie sheet

Ingredients

Dough

  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 4 tsp baking powder
  • ½ tsp salt
  • 2 tbsp brown sugar, packed
  • ½ tsp cinnamon
  • ¼ cup cold butter, small cubes
  • ⅔ cup cold milk
  • ½ tsp vanilla (optional)

Filling

  • ⅛ cup butter, melted (2 tbsp)
  • ¾ cup brown sugar, packed
  • 1½ tsp cinnamon
  • 2 tbsp cocoa powder
  • ⅓ cup mini chocolate chips (optional)

Keep It Cold

Cube the dough butter and keep it in the fridge (or freeze it to grate) until the moment you use it. Use cold milk. Work fast with your fingertips, not your palms. If the butter starts smearing or clumping, put the bowl in the fridge for 10 minutes. Chill the kneaded dough for 15 to 20 minutes before rolling. Let the melted filling butter cool for a minute or two before brushing, so it doesn't soften the dough.

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 425°F. Wipe a thin layer of oil over the pan.
  2. Filling: mix the brown sugar, cinnamon, and cocoa until the colour is even. Set it aside.
  3. Dough: in a large bowl, whisk the flour, baking powder, salt, brown sugar (break up any lumps), and cinnamon.
  4. Add the cold butter and cut it in with a fork until the butter is pilled: small, flour-coated pills and crumbs, none bigger than a pea. Don't blend it smooth.
  5. Stir the vanilla into the milk. Add about ½ cup, stir just until a shaggy dough forms, then add the rest a tablespoon at a time, only if needed.
  6. Knead gently on a lightly floured counter for about 1 minute. Wrap the dough and chill it for 15 to 20 minutes. While it chills, melt the filling butter in the microwave and let it cool.
  7. Dust the counter with a little flour and roll the dough into a rectangle about ¼ inch thick. Add a pinch more flour if it starts to stick.
  8. Brush the melted butter over the dough, leaving a bare ½-inch strip along the far long edge. Sprinkle the sugar mix evenly, add the chips if using, and press everything in firmly with your palm.
  9. Roll up from the near long edge toward the bare strip. Pinch the seam and any tears closed. Cut into 1-inch slices.
  10. Bake cut-side down on the oiled pan for 14 to 16 minutes, checking at 13. They're done when the tops and bottoms are golden and the centre is flaky, not gummy. Some filling may leak; that's normal. If the tops brown too fast, lay foil loosely over them.
